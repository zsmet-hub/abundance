THE IDEA BEHIND ABUNDANCE

Good software should
open doors.

Useful tools should be within reach. We’re building Abundance around that belief: focused desktop software that helps you get something done, then gets out of your way.

01

Free is the commitment. Everyday tools without a price, ads, or accounts standing between you and your next idea.

02

Local is the direction. Designed with offline desktop use in mind. Offline operation will be tested before each release.

03

Simple is a commitment. Clear controls, purposeful features, and a familiar feeling across the collection.
